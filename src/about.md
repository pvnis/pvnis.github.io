# About

Pavonis works on open radio access networks, FPGA and SDR hardware, and the systems software that ties them together. Most of what we do lives in the open on [GitHub]({{ site.github }}): gateware and drivers for the [LiteX M2SDR](https://github.com/pvnis/litex_m2sdr), contributions around [OCUDU](https://gitlab.com/ocudu/ocudu) and srsRAN, and tooling for running radio and GPU workloads on Kubernetes.

This blog is where we write up that work in more detail than a commit message allows.
